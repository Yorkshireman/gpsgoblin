## Current recommendation

**Next:** build a bounded **FIT activity to GPX converter** at `/tools/fit-to-gpx`, proposed in [ticket #55](https://github.com/Yorkshireman/gpsgoblin/issues/55). The owner authorised creating the ticket on 20 September; it remains open with `needs-triage`. The [original scope proposal](fit-to-gpx-ticket-proposal.md) records the investigation's recommendation; GitHub owns subsequent ticket changes. This proposes bringing conversion ahead of the baseline Stage 2 viewer release; owner acceptance is required under spec section 3.3. It does not remove FIT/TCX viewers from the intended suite.

**Why now:** the prominent example is implemented and merged. A converter would serve another concrete task for the agreed primary audience: someone already holding a FIT activity who needs its geographic recording in GPX. The completed outcome is a downloadable file. Shared decoding would also support a later FIT viewer. This is a product judgment about a bounded investment, not evidence that conversion has more searches or higher revenue than viewing.

**Evidence and uncertainty:** confidence is moderate in technical feasibility, low in acquisition and profit. Existing free competitors offer both viewing and conversion locally; privacy is a baseline, not a demonstrated advantage. Google My Maps lists GPX among supported imports and does not list FIT, establishing a plausible compatibility task, subject to destination limits and actual interoperability testing. Garmin and Strava already offer GPX export, narrowing the target to people who possess FIT files and cannot conveniently use those export paths. See the [FIT opportunity research](fit-opportunity-research.md) and [Google import requirements](https://support.google.com/mymaps/answer/3024836?hl=en). No keyword volume, attainable ranking, competitor traffic or revenue has been verified.

**Why not the alternatives:** a FIT viewer requires a useful interpretation of sessions, laps, recorded totals and sensor measurements to compete with existing viewers; copying GPX's visible fields would underserve FIT. GPX acquisition work remains valuable, but this review found no demonstrated crawling blocker, query opportunity or user failure that identifies a specific improvement likely to outperform a new file task. Missing analytics is not evidence that discovery work is unnecessary. A verified indexing problem or existing query opportunity could change this ranking.

**Done means:** the proposed converter passes its preservation, interoperability, recovery, privacy and desktop/mobile acceptance checks and is separately approved for release. Commercial success means additional useful visits ultimately contribute revenue exceeding operating costs; a release or download alone does not prove that. No profit uplift is forecast.

## Monetisation and assessment plan

Google's [AdSense eligibility guidance](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724?hl=en) and [site-readiness guidance](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7299563?hl=en) emphasise original useful content, audience and usable navigation. They do not establish a required tool count or guarantee approval. [Google's consent requirements](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13554020?hl=en) must be assessed before advertising in affected regions. This investigation neither applies for AdSense nor claims legal compliance. Advertising readiness should be revisited at the next release checkpoint, rather than deferred until the entire suite exists.

For the selected investment, record launch date, tested scope and known defects. Use existing Search Console access if available to record a pre-release baseline and then review indexing, queries, impressions and clicks for the converter and GPX page after approximately **four weeks of public availability**, with a subsequent review around eight weeks if observations are sparse. These are review windows, not automatic failure thresholds or deadlines. [Search Console's performance report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en) supplies search metrics, not successful conversions. If access stays unavailable, label acquisition impact unknown and use permissioned task observations and reported interoperability outcomes; do not require new tracking.

Assess successful downloading/opening separately from acquisition, using representative synthetic/permissioned files and, when available, voluntary user feedback. When ads are separately approved and active, review actual revenue and domain/hosting/map/other costs over matching periods. Record maintenance effort separately. Reassess earlier for correctness failures, confirmed indexing blockers, decoder/licence incompatibility, significant supplier changes or clear evidence favouring another task. Do not expand formats, batch conversion or FIT analysis solely because this release is technically complete.

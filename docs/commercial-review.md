# Commercial review

**Last reviewed:** 20 September 2026. **Status:** [ticket #55](https://github.com/Yorkshireman/gpsgoblin/issues/55) remains open, but its decoder dependency gate is not currently satisfied. The alternative TCX-to-GPX investigation found a technically feasible local converter but insufficient commercial evidence to justify a delivery ticket. No commercial performance dataset was accessible.

The [product spec](product-spec.md), especially sections 2, 3.3 and 23, owns strategy. This record follows the [commercial review workflow](../AGENTS.md#commercial-project-management-what-next). Recommendations do not authorise implementation, publishing, spending, telemetry or merging.

## Current recommendation

**Next:** do not begin either converter. Defer the blocked FIT work in #55 and do not create a TCX-to-GPX delivery ticket without new evidence of a recurring source/destination compatibility task. Prefer an evidenced GPX acquisition or task-completion correction when Search Console, a user report or a confirmed defect supplies one. Until then, avoid inventing an SEO page or filling the gap with optional feature work.

**Why now:** TCX avoids FIT's binary-profile dependency and can use GPSGoblin's existing streaming XML parser and worker architecture without a paid service. That lower engineering risk does not establish commercial value: Garmin Connect, Strava and Polar Flow already export GPX directly; several common destinations accept TCX; and multiple free browser-local converters already offer the same basic task and privacy position.

**Evidence and uncertainty:** Google My Maps supplies one real gap because it accepts GPX but not TCX, but no reviewed evidence establishes how often that task occurs or whether GPSGoblin could acquire it. Search volume, attainable rankings, completed conversions, advertising yield and profit remain unknown. Garmin publishes the TCX schemas without an explicit redistribution licence; a future implementation could avoid bundling or generating from them, but this investigation does not provide legal clearance. See the [TCX feasibility research](tcx-to-gpx-feasibility-research.md), [FIT package audit](fit-file-parser-license-research.md) and [remaining FIT decoder review](fit-decoder-options-research.md).

**Why not the alternatives:** another FIT package repeats the completed provenance problem. TCX implementation would spend design, fixture and compatibility effort in a crowded category without a demonstrated acquisition route. GPX sharing, 3D replay and richer analysis deepen the product but do not address the working acquisition constraint. Advertising remains disabled and has separate audience, consent and policy gates; enabling it is not a substitute for useful traffic.

**Done means:** keep #55 deferred and the TCX converter unticketed until a named source/destination pair, existing Search Console evidence or a permissioned user report clears the commercial gate. A future TCX ticket must also preserve activities, courses, multisport structure and disclosed losses without vendoring Garmin schemas. Technical completion would still not prove demand or profit.

## Monetisation and assessment plan

Google's [AdSense eligibility guidance](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724?hl=en) and [site-readiness guidance](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7299563?hl=en) emphasise original useful content, audience and usable navigation. They do not establish a required tool count or guarantee approval. [Google's consent requirements](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13554020?hl=en) must be assessed before advertising in affected regions. This investigation neither applies for AdSense nor claims legal compliance. Advertising readiness should be revisited at the next release checkpoint, rather than deferred until the entire suite exists.

For the selected investment, record launch date, tested scope and known defects. Use existing Search Console access if available to record a pre-release baseline and then review indexing, queries, impressions and clicks for the converter and GPX page after approximately **four weeks of public availability**, with a subsequent review around eight weeks if observations are sparse. These are review windows, not automatic failure thresholds or deadlines. [Search Console's performance report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en) supplies search metrics, not successful conversions. If access stays unavailable, label acquisition impact unknown and use permissioned task observations and reported interoperability outcomes; do not require new tracking.

Assess successful downloading/opening separately from acquisition, using representative synthetic/permissioned files and, when available, voluntary user feedback. When ads are separately approved and active, review actual revenue and domain/hosting/map/other costs over matching periods. Record maintenance effort separately. Reassess earlier for correctness failures, confirmed indexing blockers, decoder/licence incompatibility, significant supplier changes or clear evidence favouring another task. Do not expand formats, batch conversion or FIT analysis solely because this release is technically complete.

## Decision history

- **20 September 2026 — TCX converter not justified:** bounded research found TCX-to-GPX technically feasible with the existing XML parser, worker and static architecture, but common direct GPX exports, broad TCX import support and strong free local competition leave no evidenced acquisition advantage. No delivery ticket was created. Reconsider only for a named recurring compatibility gap, relevant Search Console evidence or an adjacent approved TCX viewer that materially changes incremental cost. [Research](tcx-to-gpx-feasibility-research.md).
- **20 September 2026 — decoder gate failed pending a new decision:** exact review rejected `fit-file-parser@5.2.1` as an MIT-cleared workaround because its complete profile is generated from Garmin's SDK. A wider review found no maintained browser-ready dependency with both defensible profile provenance and ticket-level correctness. Recommend deferring public FIT work unless the owner explicitly approves a time-boxed independent narrow-decoder spike. This changes implementation readiness, not the agreed V1 format ambition. [Package audit](fit-file-parser-license-research.md); [remaining options](fit-decoder-options-research.md).
- **20 September 2026 — preview decision:** owner agreed that #55 should offer Preview converted GPX within the converter using the existing GPX viewer, with Download GPX visible throughout and source/output retained on close. This addresses uncertainty about losing the download while inspecting it; no task-completion or profit uplift is yet observed. Requirements are in [spec section 8.1](product-spec.md#81-fitgpx-and-tcxgpx) and the [ticket scope](fit-to-gpx-ticket-proposal.md). Only documentation and ticket updates are delivered here; the converter remains unimplemented.
- **20 September 2026 — ticket created:** owner authorised publishing [#55](https://github.com/Yorkshireman/gpsgoblin/issues/55), verified open with `needs-triage`. Implementation, release and converter-first sequencing remain pending. Clarified that passing basic crawling and indexing configuration checks does not establish Google indexing, rankings or traffic.
- **19 September 2026 — post-example review:** #46 verified merged with public entry points/asset present. Recommend a scoped FIT activity-to-GPX release, pending owner acceptance of sequencing and scope; FIT viewer and GPX acquisition remain alternatives. Commercial outcome unobserved. [Research](fit-opportunity-research.md); [ticket proposal](fit-to-gpx-ticket-proposal.md).
- **19 September 2026 — example first:** owner fixed #46 as the next implementation priority. It was delivered through PR #50; documentation PRs #47/#48 alone did not satisfy it.
- **19 September 2026 — revised commercial strategy:** owner accepted the review of PR #48. Replace the open-ended GPX-depth/export-incentive gate with evidence-backed opportunity selection. Primary audience is people already holding files; comparative profitability remains a hypothesis.
- **19 September 2026 — objective and management:** optimise sustainable total operating profit. Development effort is secondary; recurring cash costs and quality boundaries remain material. Reviews are on demand, with monthly evidence refreshes and meaningful release checkpoints; no automation is created.
